How to play

  1. 1Hide your ships. Place 12 ships on your Fleet Board.
  2. 2Attack at the same time! No waiting for turns. Click a square on the Attack Board and find the slope between the two points.
  3. 3Type your answer, then click Fire. Correct answer = your shot is fired. Wrong answer = wait 20 seconds, then try again.
  4. 4Be the first to sink all 12 ships to win!
